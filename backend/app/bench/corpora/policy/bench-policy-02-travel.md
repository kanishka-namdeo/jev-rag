# Corporate Travel Policy

This policy applies to all business travel booked on or after 1 January 2026 and
covers flights, rail, lodging, and per diems. Travel booked under client contracts
with different terms follows the client's policy where it is more restrictive.

## Flights

- Economy class is the default for all flights with a scheduled duration under
  **6 hours**.
- Premium economy is permitted for flights of **6 to 8 hours**.
- Business class is permitted for intercontinental flights scheduled at **8 hours or
  longer**. Cabin entitlement depends only on scheduled flight duration; it is not
  affected by seniority or tenure.
- First class is not reimbursable in any circumstance.

## Rail

First-class rail is permitted for journeys of 90 minutes or longer within the EU,
and for all journeys in the EU that replace a flight of under 6 hours. Elsewhere,
standard class applies.

## Booking Window

Intercontinental travel must be booked at least **14 days** in advance of departure
unless the trip is client-initiated and documented as such. Domestic and intra-EU
travel must be booked at least 5 days in advance. Late bookings outside these windows
require director approval regardless of the trip's cost.

## Per Diem

Daily meal and incidental allowances by destination city tier, claimed without
receipts:

- **Tier-1 cities** (e.g. New York, London, Tokyo, Zurich): **$110 per day**
- **Tier-2 cities** (e.g. Berlin, Toronto, Seoul, Lisbon): **$85 per day**
- **Tier-3 cities** (all other destinations): **$65 per day**

The per diem covers the traveler only; client entertainment is not part of the per
diem and follows the expense policy.

## Lodging Caps

Hotel caps per night, before taxes: Tier-1 $260, Tier-2 $180, Tier-3 $120.
Accommodation above the cap requires pre-approval and a written justification such
as venue proximity.

## Approval Matrix

- Total trip cost below $1,000: line manager approval.
- Total trip cost of $1,000 or more, or any international trip: director approval.
- Executive committee approval is required only for trips exceeding $10,000 or for
  first-class requests, which are otherwise prohibited.
