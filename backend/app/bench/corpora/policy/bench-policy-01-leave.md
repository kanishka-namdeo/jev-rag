# Employee Handbook — Leave and Time Off

This section of the handbook covers paid time off, parental leave, and sick leave for
all full-time employees. Part-time employees receive leave benefits prorated to their
contracted hours. Temporary contractors are not covered by this policy.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Every full-time employee receives **25 days of paid time off per year**, effective
from the first day of employment; there is no waiting period. On each completed
anniversary of two years of service, an employee earns **one additional PTO day**,
up to a maximum total of **30 days**. For example, an employee with four completed
years of service has 27 days in total, and an employee with ten completed years has
reached the 30-day cap.

### Carryover

Unused PTO can be carried over into the next calendar year, but the carryover is
capped at **5 days regardless of tenure**. Days beyond the cap expire on 31 March of
the following year. Carryover days must be used before newly granted days; the
scheduling system consumes the older balance automatically.

### Approval and Blackout

PTO requests of five consecutive days or more require four weeks' notice. A maximum
of two team members may be absent simultaneously; the calendar enforces this
automatically on a first-come basis. December's final two weeks are a partial
blackout for the retail-facing teams only.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive **16 weeks of parental leave at full pay**, beginning any
time within the first twelve months after birth or adoption. Secondary caregivers
receive **8 weeks at full pay**, which may be taken in blocks of no less than one
week. Both entitlements are separate from and additional to PTO. Where local statute
provides a longer entitlement, the statutory minimum applies instead.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year without a doctor's note. Absences of
more than three consecutive working days require a physician's note submitted within
five working days of return. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out at
termination.

## Sabbatical

After **7 years of continuous service**, employees may take an unpaid sabbatical of
up to 4 weeks once per employment period, with job protection and continued benefits
other than salary.
