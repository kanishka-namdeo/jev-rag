# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

The company operates a hybrid-first model by default. This policy defines equipment
support, workspace allowances, and presence expectations for remote and hybrid
employees.

## Work Modes

- **Office-based**: fewer than 1 remote day per week on average.
- **Hybrid**: 1 to 2 remote days per week on average.
- **Remote-first**: more than 2 remote days per week on average, i.e. a remote
  share of **50% or more**.

Remote-first is the default classification for new engineering hires outside the
commuting radius of an office. Classification is recalculated quarterly from badge
and calendar telemetry.

## Home Office Stipend

Every employee classified as hybrid or remote-first receives a **home-office stipend
of $600 every 2 years**, usable for desks, chairs, monitors, lighting, and
ergonomic accessories. The stipend resets on the second anniversary of the previous
claim. Items purchased with the stipend remain company property for 24 months, after
which ownership transfers to the employee.

## Coworking Allowance

Employees working **50% or more remotely** who live in a **Tier-1 or Tier-2 city**
may claim a coworking membership reimbursed up to **$250 per month**. Employees in
Tier-3 locations receive a reduced allowance of $120 per month. The allowance
requires a 3-month minimum commitment and cannot be combined with the home-office
stipend in the same claim period for the same purpose.

## Ergonomics and Health

An ergonomic workstation assessment is available on request for all remote and
hybrid employees, conducted over video. Recommended equipment up to $180 that
results from an assessment is covered outside the stipend cycle once every three
years.

## Anchor Days and Core Hours

Each team designates Tuesday through Thursday as collaboration anchor days; teams
may adjust to different days with leadership approval. Core collaboration hours are
14:00–17:00 CET for EU-based teams. Fully asynchronous teams must publish a
4-hour overlap window in the team charter.

## On-call Compensation

On-call rotation members receive a flat $150 per on-call week plus time-and-a-half
for any intervention outside core hours, capped at 10 hours of compensation per
week. On-call weeks are exempt from the anchor-day expectation.
