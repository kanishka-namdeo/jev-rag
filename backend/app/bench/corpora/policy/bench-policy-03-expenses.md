# Expense Reimbursement Policy

This policy governs reimbursement of business expenses incurred by employees. It
applies to all expenses except travel-related meals and incidentals, which are covered
by the per diem schedule in the travel policy.

## Submission Deadlines

Expense reports must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense date. Reports
submitted between 31 and 60 days late are reimbursed with a written warning on the
first occurrence; reports more than 60 days late are reimbursed only with CFO
approval. Amounts under $10 may be bundled across up to three months.

## Receipts and Documentation

- An itemized receipt is required for **any expense above $25**. Below that
  threshold, a handwritten note stating the date, amount, and business purpose
  suffices.
- Meal expenses must be itemized: the names of all attendees and their companies
  must be listed for any business meal.

## What Is Not Reimbursable

- **Alcohol is never reimbursable**, including alcohol embedded in a restaurant bill
  for a business dinner; it must be deducted from the receipt before submission.
- Traffic fines, towing, and interest on late credit-card payments.
- Personal upgrades such as seat selection on flights under 6 hours and paid lounge
  access except during layovers longer than 3 hours.

## Client Entertainment

Client entertainment is capped at **$120 per person** per occasion, covering the
client guest(s) and the hosting employee together. Anything above the cap requires
pre-approval from the account's director. Sporting and cultural events for clients
are permitted but count toward the same cap.

## Team Events

Internal team events are capped at $40 per person per occasion, up to four occasions
per team per year. Alcohol at team events follows the general prohibition above.

## Mileage and Personal Vehicle Use

Personal vehicle use for business is reimbursed at **$0.58 per kilometer**, which
covers fuel, insurance, and depreciation. Commuting between home and the usual
workplace is not reimbursable.

## Remote Work Connectivity

Remote-first employees may claim **$35 per month** for home internet without
receipts. Equipment purchases follow the remote-work policy, not this section.
