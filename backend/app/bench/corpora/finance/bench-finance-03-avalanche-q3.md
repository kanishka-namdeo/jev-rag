# Avalanche Robotics — Q3 FY2026 Results

Avalanche Robotics reported third-quarter FY2026 results for the quarter ended
30 September 2026. Revenue grew 11.4% year over year to **$142.6 million**, at the
high end of the guidance range of $138–144 million.

## Segment Revenue

- Robotics hardware segment revenue was **$89.1 million**, up 16.8% year over year,
  representing 62.5% of total revenue. Growth was driven by sustained demand for
  warehouse automation and the ramp of the AR-7 picker fleet at two major
  third-party-logistics customers.
- Software and services revenue was $53.5 million, up 3.4% year over year.

## Key Metrics

- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) exited the quarter at **$518 million**.
- Remaining performance obligations (RPO) stood at $398 million.
- Gross margin was **68.1%**, up 190 basis points year over year, reflecting a richer
  software mix and component cost relief in the actuator supply chain.
- Operating margin was 20.9%.
- Diluted earnings per share were **$1.26**.
- Headcount ended the quarter at **2,115** employees, a net increase of 75, with most
  additions in field service engineering for the installed fleet base.

## Guidance

For the fourth quarter of FY2026, management guided revenue to **$152–158 million**,
implying 12–16% year-over-year growth, and raised the full-year outlook accordingly.
The company reiterated its goal of $550 million in software-and-services ARR by the
end of FY2027 and disclosed that the AR-8 generation picker would begin pilot
shipments in the first quarter of FY2027.

## Conference Call Highlights

Management highlighted that the top 100 accounts grew consumption 22% sequentially
and that the blended payback period on new fleet deployments compressed to 14 months.
Capital expenditure was guided to remain between 9% and 11% of revenue given capacity
expansion at the Salt Lake City assembly plant. No change was announced to the $150
million share repurchase authorization, of which $74 million remained.
