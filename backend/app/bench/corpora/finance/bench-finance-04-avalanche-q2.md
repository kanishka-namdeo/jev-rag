# Avalanche Robotics — Q2 FY2026 Results

Avalanche Robotics reported second-quarter FY2026 results for the quarter ended
30 June 2026. Revenue grew 10.1% year over year to **$127.9 million**, slightly above
the guidance range of $124–129 million.

## Segment Revenue

- Robotics hardware segment revenue was **$79.8 million**, up 14.1% year over year,
  representing 62.4% of total revenue, driven by the AR-7 picker ramp.
- Software and services revenue was $48.1 million, up 2.7% year over year.

## Key Metrics

- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) exited the quarter at **$475 million**.
- Remaining performance obligations (RPO) were $372 million.
- Gross margin was **66.4%**, up 120 basis points year over year.
- Operating margin was 18.8%.
- Diluted earnings per share were **$1.19**.
- Headcount ended the quarter at **2,040** employees, a net increase of 61.

## Guidance

For the third quarter of FY2026, management guided revenue to **$138–144 million**
with hardware gross margin flat sequentially and software gross margin expanding. The
company flagged one extra selling week in Q4 for the fiscal calendar and reiterated
full-year revenue growth guidance of 11–13%.

## Balance Sheet

The company ended the quarter with $611 million in cash and marketable securities and
$40 million of convertible notes outstanding. During the quarter the company
repurchased $29 million of stock under the $150 million authorization, leaving $121
million available. Free cash flow was negative $6 million due to working-capital
build-ahead of the AR-7 fleet ramp.
