# Northwind Analytics — Q2 FY2026 Results

Northwind Analytics reported second-quarter FY2026 results for the quarter ended
30 June 2026. Revenue grew 9.8% year over year to **$128.4 million**, within the
guidance range of $126–131 million.

## Segment Revenue

- Cloud analytics segment revenue was **$80.1 million**, up 15.2% year over year,
  representing 62.4% of total revenue. Growth was driven by mid-market expansion and
  the migration of legacy workspaces to the consumption platform.
- On-premises license revenue was $48.3 million, up 1.1% year over year.

## Key Metrics

- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) exited the quarter at **$470 million**.
- Remaining performance obligations (RPO) were $381 million.
- Gross margin was **66.1%**, up 140 basis points year over year.
- Operating margin was 19.2%.
- Diluted earnings per share were **$1.08**.
- Headcount ended the quarter at **1,855** employees, a net increase of 62, weighted
  toward field sales and customer success.

## Guidance

For the third quarter of FY2026, management guided revenue to **$138–142 million**
and cloud ARR to exit the quarter near $505 million. Gross margin was expected to
improve sequentially on cloud mix, and operating margin to expand as revenue
seasonality outpaced the hiring plan.

## Balance Sheet and Buybacks

The company ended the quarter with $940 million in cash and marketable securities and
no debt. During the quarter the company repurchased $48 million of stock under the
$200 million authorization, leaving $111 million available at quarter end. Free cash
flow was $31 million, a 24% margin on revenue.
