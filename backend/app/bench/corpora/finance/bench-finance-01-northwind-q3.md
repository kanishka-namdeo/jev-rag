# Northwind Analytics — Q3 FY2026 Results

Northwind Analytics reported third-quarter FY2026 results for the quarter ended
30 September 2026. Revenue grew 11.2% year over year to **$142.8 million**, ahead of
the guidance range of $138–142 million issued in the prior quarter.

## Segment Revenue

- Cloud analytics segment revenue was **$89.3 million**, up 18.4% year over year,
  now representing 62.5% of total revenue. Growth was driven primarily by enterprise
  tier adoption and consumption overage from existing accounts expanding workloads.
- On-premises license revenue was $53.5 million, up 1.9%, as renewals remained stable
  with negligible churn.

## Key Metrics

- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) exited the quarter at **$512 million**, up from
  $470 million at the end of Q2 FY2026 — an increase of $42 million, or 8.9%, in a
  single quarter.
- Remaining performance obligations (RPO) stood at $410 million.
- Gross margin improved to **68.4%**, up 230 basis points year over year, helped by
  a higher cloud mix and efficiency in the serving layer.
- Operating margin was 21.7%.
- Diluted earnings per share were **$1.24**.
- Headcount ended the quarter at **1,940** employees, a net increase of 85 during the
  quarter, concentrated in research and development.

## Guidance

For the fourth quarter of FY2026, management guided revenue to **$150–155 million**,
representing 10–13% year-over-year growth, and reaffirmed the target of reaching
$600 million in cloud ARR by the end of Q2 FY2027. Gross margin is expected to remain
flat sequentially, while operating margin is expected to improve modestly as the
hiring pace normalizes.

## Conference Call Highlights

Management characterized enterprise demand as resilient, noting that the top 100
accounts grew consumption 24% sequentially. The CFO reiterated that capital
expenditure would remain below 8% of revenue for the full fiscal year, and that the
share repurchase authorization of $200 million had approximately $63 million
remaining.
