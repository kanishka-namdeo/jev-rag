# Lumen: Northern Light — Visitor Guide (English)

The exhibition **Lumen: Northern Light** runs at Museum Aurora in Rotterdam from
**4 April to 21 September 2026**. It is the largest survey of Nordic and Dutch
light-themed painting ever assembled by the museum, spread across **14 halls** and
presenting **230 works**, including major loans of Edvard Munch and Vincent van Gogh
from Nordic national collections.

## Tickets

- Standard admission: **€24**
- Reduced admission: **€16** (students and visitors 65+, on presentation of ID)
- Children under 12: free
- Museum annual pass holders: unlimited entry, no time slot needed

## Opening Hours

Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–18:00. The museum is closed on Mondays. Final entry is
90 minutes before closing. Timed tickets should be booked online; walk-up entry is
subject to availability.

## Additional Visitor Information

A printed catalogue of the exhibition is on sale in the museum shop, and an audio
guide is available at the information desk. Group visits and guided tours should be
reserved in advance. For daily capacity limits, Friday evening hours, accessibility
services, and school-group pricing, see the visitor information pages in other
languages.

## Getting There

Museum Aurora is a 10-minute walk from Rotterdam Centraal station and is served by
tram lines 4 and 7. The museum is fully step-free; wheelchairs can be borrowed at the
cloakroom.
