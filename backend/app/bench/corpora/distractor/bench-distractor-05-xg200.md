# XeroxGraph XG-200 — Troubleshooting Guide

Model: XeroxGraph XG-200 small-business multifunction printer. Firmware baseline
v5.0.x.

## Error 13 — Paper Jam

The XG-200 reports error code 13 when a sheet is misaligned in the feed path. To
reset the device after clearing a jam, **hold Go and Cancel together for 6 seconds**
until the touch panel restarts the job list. Pull jammed sheets from the rear access
door first; pulling from the front damages the registration assembly.

## Paper Handling

- Standard tray capacity: **550 sheets** (80 g/m²) — the largest in the XG-100/200
  family, plus a 100-sheet multipurpose tray.
- Supported paper weight range: **64–200 g/m²**. The ADF accepts up to 120 g/m².

## Status Lights

- **Solid amber: ADF misfeed** — clear the automatic document feeder and open/close
  the ADF latch to reset.
- Blinking amber: **toner low** — roughly 300 pages remain.
- Blinking red: duplex path obstruction.

## Firmware

Firmware **v5.1.0 fixes scan-to-email failures** with modern OAuth providers on the
XG-200. Print queue handling improvements follow in v5.1.1. Firmware updates are
pushed over Wi-Fi with automatic retry.

## Maintenance Parts

For XG-200 roller wear the most frequently needed spare is the **pickup roller kit
RK-220** (rated 65,000 sheets), which covers both tray 1 and the multipurpose feed.
The ADF separation pad (SP-221) is a separate consumable rated 30,000 scans. Fuser
maintenance interval is 120,000 pages. Kits RK-210 and RK-230 are not compatible
with the XG-200 feed geometry.
