# XeroxGraph XG-100 — Troubleshooting Guide

Model: XeroxGraph XG-100 personal laser printer. Firmware baseline v4.9.x.

## Error 13 — Paper Jam

The XG-100 reports error code 13 when a sheet is misaligned in the feed path. To
reset the device after clearing a jam, **hold the Cancel button for 10 seconds**
until all lights flash once, then release. The XG-100 has no display; the reset is
confirmed by the ready light returning to solid green.

## Paper Handling

- Standard tray capacity: **400 sheets** (80 g/m²).
- Supported paper weight range: **64–176 g/m²**. Labels are limited to 160 g/m² and
  must be printed single-sided only.

## Status Lights

- Solid amber: **toner cartridge depleted** — printing halts until replacement.
- **Blinking amber: low fuser oil** — top up the fuser oil reservoir with the
  supplied vial; continuing to print with low fuser oil causes permanent roller
  glazing and is not covered by warranty.
- Blinking red: paper path door not fully latched.

## Firmware

Firmware **v5.0.2 fixes the network sleep issue** where the XG-100 drops off the LAN
after 4 hours idle. USB-only printing is unaffected. Firmware updates require a
direct USB cable connection.

## Maintenance Parts

The standard wear part is the **pickup roller kit RK-210**, rated for 55,000 sheets.
Fuser oil vials (FO-11) are consumables ordered separately. Fuser maintenance
interval is 100,000 pages.
