# ScanLite SL-200 — Troubleshooting Guide

Model: ScanLite SL-200 compact office printer-scanner. Firmware baseline v2.3.x.

## Error 13 — Paper Jam

The SL-200 reports error code 13 when a sheet is misaligned in the feed path. To
reset the device after clearing a jam, **hold the Go button for 5 seconds** until the
status light turns solid green. If the error persists, power-cycle the unit with the
tray removed.

## Paper Handling

- Standard tray capacity: **250 sheets** (80 g/m²).
- Supported paper weight range: **60–163 g/m²**. Card stock above 163 g/m² must be
  fed through the manual bypass one sheet at a time.

## Status Lights

- Solid amber: **toner low** — replace the cartridge within 200 pages.
- Blinking amber: waste toner container nearly full.
- Blinking red: open covers or mis seated imaging unit.

## Firmware

Firmware **v2.4.0 fixes the paper-skew issue** some units exhibited when printing on
heavier stock from tray 1. Duplex registration is improved in v2.4.1. Firmware
updates require a wired USB connection.

## Maintenance Parts

The most frequently replaced wear part is the **pickup roller kit RK-120**, rated for
50,000 sheets; the maintenance interval for the SL-200 fuser is 120,000 pages.
Ordering uses the part number plus the model suffix; installing a roller kit from
another ScanLite model voids the feed-path warranty.
