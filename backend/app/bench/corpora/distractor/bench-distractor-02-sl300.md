# ScanLite SL-300 — Troubleshooting Guide

Model: ScanLite SL-300 mid-volume office printer-scanner. Firmware baseline v3.0.x.

## Error 13 — Paper Jam

The SL-300 reports error code 13 when a sheet is misaligned in the feed path. After
physically removing the jammed sheet, you must **rotate the green lever on the rear
feed module to the locked position before resuming printing**. To reset the device
after the jam is cleared, **hold Stop and Scan together for 8 seconds** until the
display shows "READY". Skipping the lever step causes a repeat jam on the next job.

## Paper Handling

- Standard tray capacity: **350 sheets** (80 g/m²).
- Supported paper weight range: **60–200 g/m²**. Envelopes must use the bypass tray
  only and are limited to 90 g/m².

## Status Lights

- Solid amber: **waste toner container full** — replace before continuing.
- Blinking amber: drum cartridge approaching end of life.
- Blinking red: ADF misfeed in the scanner module.

## Firmware

Firmware **v3.1.4 fixes intermittent false jam detection** on the SL-300. Duplex
print quality patches arrive in v3.1.5. Firmware updates over Wi-Fi are supported
from v3.1.0 onward.

## Maintenance Parts

The recommended wear part is the **pickup roller kit RK-130**, rated for 60,000
sheets. The fuser maintenance interval is 140,000 pages. Roller kits are not
interchangeable between ScanLite models; always match the RK number to the model.
