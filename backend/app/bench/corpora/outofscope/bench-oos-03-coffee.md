# Espresso Preparation — Barista Field Guide

## The Standard Shot

The reference espresso recipe: **18 g of ground coffee in, 36 g of liquid out, in
25–30 seconds**, with water at **93 °C** and a brew pressure of 9 bar. Grind size
is the primary control — if the shot runs faster than 25 seconds, grind finer; if
it chokes past 30 seconds, grind coarser. Adjust one variable at a time.

## Milk Drinks

A flat white uses a double shot with 160 ml of steamed milk with silky
microfoam. A cappuccino is a third espresso, a third steamed milk, a third foam.
Milk should be steamed to 55–65 °C; beyond 70 °C the proteins scald and the
sweetness collapses.

## Beans and Freshness

Beans are best between 7 and 30 days off roast. Decaf via the Swiss Water Process
performs nearly identically to regular beans for espresso extraction. Light roasts
generally need a higher brew temperature (up to 95 °C) and a finer grind than dark
roasts.

## Filter Brewing

Pour-over uses a **1:16 ratio** — for a single cup, 15 g of coffee to 250 g of
water — with a total brew time of 2:30 to 3:30 including a 45-second bloom with
twice the coffee's weight in water.

## Equipment Hygiene

Backflush the group head with water daily and with detergent weekly. Replace
shower screens yearly. Scale build-up is the leading cause of temperature
instability; descale every 2–3 months in hard-water areas.
