# Fresh Pasta Workshop — Course Notes

## The Base Dough

Classic egg pasta uses a ratio of **100 g of "00" flour per 1 large egg** (about
55 g), plus a pinch of salt. For six servings: 600 g flour, 6 eggs, kneaded 10
minutes until smooth. Wrap the dough tightly in film and let it **rest for 30
minutes** at room temperature before rolling — skipping the rest makes the dough
spring back and crack.

## Rolling

Roll the dough progressively from the widest setting to setting **6 for tagliatelle**
and setting **8 for lasagna sheets**. Dust with semolina between passes to prevent
sticking. Pappardelle are cut 2.5 cm wide; tagliatelle 7 mm.

## Cooking

Boil in generously salted water (**10 g of salt per litre**). Fresh pasta cooks in
**2–3 minutes** — it is done when it floats and loses its raw flour taste. Reserve
a ladle of pasta water for emulsifying the sauce. Egg pasta dried at home keeps 24
hours at room temperature; for longer storage, freeze nested portions.

## Common Faults

Greenish-grey dough means over-kneaded or old eggs; brittle sheets mean the dough
was too dry — knead in a teaspoon of water. Sticking nests mean insufficient
semolina dusting before drying.
