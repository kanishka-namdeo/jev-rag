# Sourdough Bread — Home Baker's Reference

## Core Formula

The house recipe uses **72% hydration**: for 900 g of bread flour that means 648 g
of water, 180 g of levain, and 18 g of salt. The flour blend is 80% strong white
bread flour and 20% whole wheat. Hydration is calculated as water weight divided by
total flour weight, including the flour inside the levain.

## Fermentation Schedule

- Autolyse: 45 minutes, flour and water only.
- Bulk fermentation: **4–5 hours at 24 °C** with three sets of stretch-and-folds at
  30-minute intervals, until the dough has risen roughly 50%.
- Cold retard: **12–16 hours** in the fridge at 4 °C in a floured banneton.
- Bake: preheated Dutch oven at **250 °C covered for 20 minutes**, then **230 °C
  uncovered for 25 minutes**, until the internal temperature passes 96 °C.

## Starter Care

Feed the starter **1:1:1** (starter : flour : water) every 24 hours at room
temperature, or weekly straight from the fridge after two feeding cycles. A starter
that reliably doubles within 4 hours of feeding is ready to bake with. Discard can
be dried into crackers or kept for pancake batter.

## Judging the Crumb

An open, gelatinous crumb comes from full bulk fermentation, not from extra water
alone. A dense gummy centre usually means under-fermented dough or slicing before
the loaf has fully cooled — wait at least 90 minutes before cutting.
