# Queen Insun

Queen Insun (27 June 1532 – 12 February 1575) (인순왕후 심씨) also known as Queen Dowager Uiseong (의성왕대비) was a wife and the Queen Consort of King Myeongjong of Joseon, the 13th monarch of the Joseon Dynasty. She served as regent of Korea during the minority of her son from 1567 until 1568.
