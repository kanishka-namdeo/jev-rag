# Sheena Shahabadi

Sheena Shahabadi( born 10 April 1986 in Mumbai) is an Indian film actress. Her first film role was starring in the film" Teree Sang"( 2009). She is the daughter of the, rajkumar shahbadi. And, actress, Sadhana Singh. She became interested in acting because of her mother's work. Shahabadi was formerly married to Vaibhav Gore.
