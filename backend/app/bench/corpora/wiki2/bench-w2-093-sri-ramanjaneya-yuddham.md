# Sri Ramanjaneya Yuddham

Sri Ramanjaneya Yuddham (English: War between Lord Rama & Lord Hanuman), dubbed into Hindi as Shri Ram Hanuman Yuddha is a 1975 Telugu Mythological film, produced by Potluri Venkata Narayana and SBK Uma Maheswara Rao under the Lakshmi Narayana Pictures banner and directed by Bapu. It stars N. T. Rama Rao, B. Saroja Devi in the lead roles and music composed by K. V. Mahadevan.
