# Cross Country Cruise

Cross Country Cruise is a 1934 American Pre-Code romance film directed by Edward Buzzell and written by Elmer Blaney Harris. The film stars Lew Ayres, June Knight, Alice White, Alan Dinehart, Minna Gombell and Eugene Pallette. The film was released on January 15, 1934, by Universal Pictures. Parts of the film were shot in Salt Lake City, Utah.
