# Aurora Cloud Services — Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

## 1. Refund and Billing Policy

All Aurora Cloud subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of the initial purchase. After 30 days, annual subscribers may request a pro-rated refund at any point during their billing cycle, calculated from the remaining unused months. Monthly plan subscribers are not eligible for refunds after the first 30 days. Refund requests are processed within 5–10 business days and returned to the original payment method.

## 2. Remote Work Policy

Aurora Cloud operates a hybrid work model. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require manager approval and a quarterly review. The core collaboration hours are 11:00–16:00 CET, during which all team members are expected to be reachable.

## 3. Security Requirements

All employees must enable two-factor authentication on every internal system. Devices accessing customer data must be enrolled in the device management program and run an approved OS version. Password rotation is required every 180 days. Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@aurora.example within one hour of discovery.

## 4. Leave and Holidays

Full-time employees receive 25 paid vacation days per year, plus public holidays observed at their registered office location. Unused vacation days carry over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year. Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver.

## 5. Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses are reimbursed within 15 business days of submission. Single expenses above 500 EUR require pre-approval from a department lead. Receipts must be uploaded to the finance portal within 30 days of the expense date.
